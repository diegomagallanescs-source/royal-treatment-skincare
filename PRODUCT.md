# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women primarily, local to the Inland Empire / Southern California area. They're looking for professional skincare treatments and products from a trusted, licensed esthetician with 30+ years of experience. Many are repeat clients booking appointments, some are new visitors discovering the studio. They arrive calm and intentional — this isn't an impulse purchase.

## Product Purpose

Royal Treatment Skincare is a boutique, one-esthetician skincare studio. The website exists to book appointments, introduce the studio's services and monthly-rotating products, and surface educational skincare content that builds trust. Success means a visitor books an appointment or calls for a consultation.

## Brand Personality

Clean, Modern, Trusted

Voice: direct and knowledgeable, never clinical or cold. The studio is professional but personal — you're seeing one specialist, not a chain.

## Anti-references

- Crowded, text-heavy spa websites with every detail above the fold
- Generic "wellness brand" aesthetics (pastel cream + sans-serif overload, stock-photo women in robes)
- SaaS-style gradient cards and hero metrics ("1000+ clients served")
- Loud promotional language ("AMAZING RESULTS", "TRANSFORM YOUR SKIN NOW")

## Design Principles

1. **Space earns trust.** White space is not emptiness — it signals confidence and professionalism. Let the brand breathe.
2. **Show less, invite more.** The hero is the door, not the pitch deck. One name, two actions, one link. Everything else is a scroll away.
3. **Monthly content drives return visits.** The Skincare Topic and Featured Product rotate each month — the Products page is the editorial hub, not just a catalog.
4. **Approachable expertise.** The tone is knowledgeable without being intimidating. A visitor should feel that the esthetician knows their stuff and will take care of them.
5. **Every path leads to a booking.** Whether landing on a product, reading about melasma, or browsing the gift card — the route to an appointment is always one click away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against background. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible. Reduced motion support on all animations via `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
